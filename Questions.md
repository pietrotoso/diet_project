<h1>List of questions</h1>

<h2>Bulk questions</h2>
1) Did the diets (in terms of quantity of single items per capita) in different countries of the world get more homogeneous in time?<br>
2) Can we see this process specifically inside specific macrogroups? (i.e cereals, pulses, meat, ecc.) <br>
3) Did the diets become more homogeneous in terms of macrogroups of items? <br>

<h3>Additional questions</h3>
4) Do we see interesting trends if we approach the bulk questions with a local lense? (a specific continent, or a specific geographic area) <br>
5) Do we see interesting trends in specific items? (Items disappearing, items strongly increasing, items that were relevant in a country becoming secondary, ecc.) <br>
6) Do we see counties with interesting trends? (Not following a potential convergence, having a suddens change, ecc.) <br>
7) Do we see moments in time were the global tendencies have a sudden change? <br>
8) Are the diets getting more homogeneous but also more diverse if taken singularly? <br>
